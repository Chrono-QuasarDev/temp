Where the water settles, and the occasion begins.

Zenala is a quiet stretch of shore built for the days that stay with you — weddings, farewells, and time away with a team. Grounds, light, and pacing. Nothing more theatrical than that.

The venue

An open lawn at the water, a pavilion for the meal, and quiet rooms set back from the path.

Every path, lawn, and table was placed so the water is part of the day — not a backdrop you glimpse between buildings. We hold three kinds of gathering, and we refuse to run them on the same clock.

If you are looking for a ballroom with a lake photo on the wall, this is not that place.
