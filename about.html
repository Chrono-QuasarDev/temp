About

The story behind the shoreline.

We did not want another hall with a lake view. We wanted the lake to be the reason people remember the day.

What does a space need to hold a wedding well in the morning, a memorial with dignity by afternoon, and a company retreat the following week?

The answer became the grounds: an open lawn that meets the water, a covered pavilion for meals and speeches, and quieter corners set back from the main paths.

Seating follows the light. Paths do not cross when two events share a day. One coordinator stays with you from first message to last guest.

Who you meet

Ama Mensah looks after the day.

Ama is your single point of contact. She has hosted family funerals and three-hundred-guest weddings on the same grounds, and she will tell you honestly if a date or layout will not work.

That honesty is the service. Everything else — chairs, timing, caterers — follows from it.

See it for yourself.
