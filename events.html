Events

For every gathering, a fitting setting.

Three occasions share the Zenala grounds, and each is planned on its own terms.

01 — Weddings

Vows by the water

The ceremony lawn sits where the grounds meet the lake, arranged so every seat faces the water. Receptions move to the pavilion or stay under open sky, depending on the season and the size of your party.

  • — Ceremony lawn with lake-facing arrangement
  • — Covered pavilion for reception & dining
  • — Capacity up to 300 guests
  • — Evening lighting along the shoreline paths

02 — Funerals

A calm setting for remembrance

Zenala offers private grounds for funerals and memorial gatherings, set apart from the main event paths. The pace is unhurried, the spaces are quiet, and there is room enough for extended family to gather comfortably.

  • — Private, screened section of the grounds
  • — Seating for family and guests, indoor or shaded
  • — Quiet rooms available for family before the service
  • — Support with catering and logistics on request
Enquire About Funerals
03 — Corporate Getaways

A change of setting, not a change of pace

For teams that need to think differently, Zenala offers meeting space with the lake as backdrop rather than a screensaver. Retreats, offsites, and team days are arranged with structure when you need it and open space when you don't.

  • — Indoor meeting space with outdoor breakout areas
  • — Wi-Fi, projection, and power throughout
  • — Team activity space along the grounds
  • — Catered lunches and evening dinners on request
