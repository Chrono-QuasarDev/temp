How the grounds work

You bring the occasion. We hold the place.

Most days here are not a “wedding package” or a “corporate package.” Someone hires the shoreline — lawn, pavilion, fire, rooms — and the day takes the shape it needs. Below are the gatherings we already know well. If yours is not named, it still belongs on the form.

01 Parties

Birthdays, after-parties, and the nights that run long.

Music on the lawn. Tables in the pavilion. Lights along the water. This is the version of Zenala most people meet first — a private ground that can take a crowd without feeling like a club.

  • Best for Birthdays, launches, reunions, after-parties
  • Guests 40–300, depending on layout
  • Held Pavilion, lawn, deck — indoor rain plan
  • Hours Afternoon into late evening, by agreement

02 Fire nights

Stories by the fire. The lake as the other guest.

Smaller, slower evenings — storytelling, acoustic sets, family circles, retreat nights. The fire sits where the lawn meets the dark. No stage plot required. Just enough light to see faces.

  • Best for Storytelling, song, intimate dinners, retreat evenings
  • Guests Usually 15–80
  • Held Fire circle, lawn edge, quiet rooms if it turns
  • Tone Unamplified unless you ask otherwise

03 Weddings

Vows facing the water. The meal wherever the light is kind.

Ceremony on the lawn, reception in the pavilion or under sky. We do not sell a template. We hold the ground, the timing, and one coordinator so the day is not rushed through someone else’s run-of-show.

  • Best for Ceremonies, receptions, traditional + white wedding days
  • Guests Up to 300
  • Held Lake-facing lawn, dining pavilion, evening paths
  • Note Weekend dates in Nov–Dec go first

04 Memorials

A screened part of the grounds, on an unhurried clock.

Funerals and memorial gatherings sit apart from party paths. Quiet rooms for family. Shade. Space for whoever needs to be there. We will not run this on the same pacing as a Saturday wedding.

  • Best for Funerals, memorials, thanksgiving services
  • Guests 40–200
  • Held Private section, indoor or shaded seating
  • Support Catering and logistics on request

05 Teams

Offsites that still get work done.

Meeting space, power, Wi-Fi, breakouts on the lawn, lunch in the pavilion. A change of setting — not a day lost to travel theatre.

  • Best for Offsites, retreats, workshops, board days
  • Guests Typically up to 80 in session
  • Held Pavilion + outdoor breakouts
  • Kit Projection, power, reliable connection
