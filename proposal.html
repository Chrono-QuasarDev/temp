Why now

Right now, Zenala’s first impression lives on borrowed ground.

Zenala has already opened its shoreline to weddings, farewells, and corporate retreats. The setting is doing its job. But people still discover it on Instagram — a platform Zenala doesn’t own and can’t fully control.

Someone searching for a wedding venue, a memorial ground, or a retreat usually decides in seconds. Today that decision happens on a feed built for scrolling, not booking. No dedicated way to check a date. No room to show the grounds. No permanent address for the name once a post scrolls away.

The structure

Six pages, each doing one job well.

Rather than one long page trying to speak to everyone at once, the site is organised so each visitor finds their answer quickly — whoever they are and whatever they’re planning.
